05 — Dampukht, Step by Step

The dampukht way.

Dampukht means cooking in its own steam — sealed, slow and low. That is the whole method, and the whole reason the platter tastes the way it does.

  1. Step 01

    Layered & sealed

    Meat and rice go into the pot in layers with whole spices, and the lid is sealed so nothing escapes.

  2. Step 02

    Slow steam

    The pot cooks on low heat in its own steam. No rushing, no stirring — the meat turns tender and the rice takes the flavour.

  3. Step 03

    Opened at the table

    The seal comes off when the platter is served — hot, fragrant and ready for the whole table to dig into.
